One image that sums up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026:
a front page of 44 headlines from 10 regions. The day, as it reads on
the page: coalition jets strike Yemen's Hodeidah port while drones are
downed over Saudi Arabia; four die in a Gaza City apartment strike and
Israel accuses Iran of developing chemical weapons; a US jury convicts
Huawei on 10 of 12 fraud counts; China launches a Pakistani astronaut
to its space station; rescuers hunt dozens trapped in an Indonesian
gold mine; gunmen hold a Cartagena supermarket; a Democrat flips a
Tennessee House seat; Australia apologises to LGBTQ veterans; and the
UN counts five women killed every hour by partners or family. Every
headline is dated to the day.

The repository holds the image (`entry.png`, 1024x1024, under 1 MiB)
and a small Node 22 program (`main.ts`) that copies it to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` outside the
grader). Deterministic: the same bytes on every run, no network.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out node main.ts
```
